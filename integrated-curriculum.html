Integrated Curriculum Programs

Bring hydroponics, sustainability, and food systems into the classroom.

Programs designed around your school’s learning goals.

Ask about Integrated Curriculum
Our approach

Overview

We design multi-week programs that embed directly into your school's existing science or interdisciplinary curriculum. Rather than running as a standalone add-on, our sessions are mapped to your learning objectives — whether that's an IPC unit on water, a FOSS module on plant biology, or a school-wide sustainability initiative. We bring the hydroponic systems, materials, and expertise; your students get hands-on growing experience that reinforces what they're already learning in class.

Standards

Alignment

We've integrated with IPC units including “On Tap” for water systems, energy and light modules, and nutrition and health units. We align to NGSS and FOSS standards where applicable. Before the program starts, we meet with your curriculum coordinator or grade-level team to map our content to your schedule and learning goals. We adjust depth, vocabulary, and assessment to match the grade level.

Flexible formats

Program formats

Programs range from 5 sessions, our minimum, to a full semester of weekly visits. A typical program is 6–8 sessions at 45–60 minutes each, delivered once per week to a single grade level. We can also run programs across multiple grade levels or classes, with adjusted scheduling. For larger engagements, we bring additional instructors and equipment.

Investment

Pricing

Starting from ¥100,000 for a five-session program, based on a ¥20,000 per-session rate. Pricing scales with program length, number of classes, and scope. All materials, equipment, and instruction are included. We present a single all-inclusive fee.
