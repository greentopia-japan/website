Who joins

Who it is for

Greentopia Labs is our flagship English-language STEM after-school program, open to children ages 6–14. It's designed for curious kids who want to get their hands dirty — whether or not they attend one of our partner schools. Labs sessions are held at our dedicated space at J's International School in Motoazabu, so families from any school in Tokyo can join.

Hands-on learning

What students do

Each session combines hydroponics, science experiments, and sustainability topics. Students plant and maintain hydroponic crops, run hands-on experiments such as testing pH, measuring light, and comparing growing methods, and explore bigger questions about where food comes from and why it matters. Activities change weekly and build over the term, so returning students go deeper each time.

Term structure

Program flow

A typical Greentopia Labs term runs 10–12 weeks. Early sessions focus on system setup and planting. Mid-term sessions introduce experimentation and data collection. Later sessions tackle sustainability themes and design challenges. The term ends with a harvest event and student presentations.

When and where

Schedule and location

Weekday afternoons, 3:00–6:00 PM, at J's International School, Motoazabu, Tokyo. Students can attend one, two, or more days per week depending on availability.

Investment

Pricing

Greentopia Labs sessions are ¥4,500 for 2 hours or ¥6,000 for 3 hours. Bulk discounts are available for families committing to multiple sessions or a full term. All materials, equipment, and instruction are included — there are no additional fees.
