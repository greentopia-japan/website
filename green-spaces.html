Our approach

Overview

Not every space needs a curriculum — some just need a tower. We install and support hydroponic tower farms and systems in homes, residential buildings, offices, coworking spaces, and community sites across Tokyo. Whether it's a single tower on your balcony or a shared rooftop farm for your building's residents, we handle the setup and help you grow.

Getting started

Simple starting point

Leases start with a 3-month trial at ¥13,000/month. After that, choose the plan that fits: ¥11,000/month if you care for the tower yourself, ¥15,000/month with a monthly service visit, or ¥18,000/month with a weekly service visit. We deliver, set up, and walk you through everything. At the end of your lease, you can renew, purchase the tower, or we pick it up and reset. No long-term commitment required.

What you get

What’s included

Every tower lease includes delivery, on-site setup, a walkthrough of basic care such as water, nutrients, and light, and end-of-lease pickup or reset. The tower runs on an automated pump and timer system — no daily maintenance is required beyond occasional nutrient-water top-ups and harvesting.

Support options

Starter kits and maintenance

New growers can add a Starter Kit, approximately ¥4,000–5,000, that includes nutrients, pH buffers, and an initial set of seedlings. Seedlings can be reordered anytime from our online form at ¥200–300 per plant. For hands-off growing, choose a plan with monthly or weekly service visits.
