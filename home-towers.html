Your tower

Overview

A hydroponic tower grows fresh greens, vegetables, herbs, and flowers right in your home — on a balcony, patio, or even in your kitchen with a grow light. No soil, no mess, and no gardening experience needed. The system runs automatically on a pump and timer; you add water and nutrients, plant your seedlings, and harvest when they are ready. It is a living kitchen garden that fits in a space smaller than one square meter.

Investment

Pricing

Leases start with a 3-month trial at ¥13,000/month. After that, choose the plan that fits: ¥11,000/month if you care for the tower yourself, ¥15,000/month with a monthly service visit, or ¥18,000/month with a weekly service visit. We offer two sizes: a 5-pot tower suitable for indoor or outdoor use, and a larger 7-pot tower for outdoor spaces. At the end of your lease, you can purchase the tower at a discounted price, renew your lease, or we will collect and reset the equipment. There is no obligation to buy.

What you get

What’s included

Delivery and on-site setup, a walkthrough of tower care and operation, automated pump and timer system, and end-of-lease pickup or reset. We set everything up and make sure you are confident before we leave.

Extras

Add-ons

Starter Kit (approximately ¥4,000–5,000): nutrients, pH buffer, and your first set of assorted seedlings. Seedlings: from ¥200 per plant, ordered anytime through our online form. Service visits: included in the monthly and weekly plans for growers who want regular professional check-ins. We will monitor plant health, top up nutrients, and troubleshoot issues.
