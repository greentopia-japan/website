Our approach

Overview

Greentopia partners with international schools across Tokyo to deliver hands-on urban farming and STEM education. Our programs bring hydroponic tower farms directly into schools — students plant, grow, monitor, and harvest real food while learning science, sustainability, and nutrition. We work alongside teachers to align every session with your existing curriculum, whether that's an IPC unit on water, a FOSS module on plant biology, or a standalone sustainability initiative. Programs run from five sessions to a full academic year.

Standards

Curriculum alignment

Our programs are designed to integrate with established frameworks including IPC (International Primary Curriculum), NGSS (Next Generation Science Standards), and FOSS (Full Option Science System). We've successfully aligned hydroponics content with units on water systems, energy and light, plant biology, nutrition, and environmental sustainability. We work with your curriculum coordinator before the program starts to map our lessons to your learning objectives — not the other way around.

The process

How it works

We bring everything: hydroponic towers, seeds, nutrients, pH kits, grow lights if needed, and all class materials. Our instructors lead each session at your school. Students do the planting, measuring, troubleshooting, and harvesting. Teachers are welcome to participate but are not required to prepare or lead — we handle the teaching. At the end of the program, students take home what they've grown, and in many programs, they build and keep a small growing system of their own.

Investment

Pricing snapshot
