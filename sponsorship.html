Why it matters

Overview

Greentopia's education and community projects can reach more people with the help of aligned supporters. Sponsorship can help schools and communities access towers, materials, workshops, and hands-on learning opportunities that might otherwise be out of reach.

Ways to contribute

How to Get Involved

You can sponsor equipment, donate towers, support a school program, fund a community workshop, provide a growing space, offer professional expertise, introduce partner organizations, or collaborate on a local project. Contact us to discuss a contribution that fits your goals.
