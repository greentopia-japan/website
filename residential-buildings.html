The concept

Overview

A tower farm in a shared residential space — rooftop, lobby, terrace, or common room — gives residents something no amenity gym or lounge can: fresh food they grew together. We design, install, and maintain hydroponic systems for apartment buildings, condominiums, and shared housing. Residents can participate in optional workshops and harvest events, or simply enjoy the greens.

What we offer

Possible formats

A single tower in a common area as an introduction. A multi-tower rooftop installation with regular programming. A seasonal grow-and-harvest program for residents with Greentopia-led workshops. Installations can be paired with ongoing maintenance contracts — we keep the farm running, your residents enjoy the results.

First step

Site assessment

Every building is different. We start with a free site visit to evaluate your space — light, water access, foot traffic, and structural considerations. From there, we propose a setup that fits your building and your residents’ interests. No commitment is required for the initial assessment.
