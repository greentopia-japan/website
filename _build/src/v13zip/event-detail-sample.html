Sample Hydroponics Workshop

A hands-on hydroponics workshop for families and curious growers. Learn how tower farms work, plant your own seedlings, and take home a small growing kit. No experience needed — just show up ready to get your hands wet.

What to expect

Program description

We start with a brief introduction to hydroponics and how tower farms work (15 minutes), then move into hands-on planting — you will set up seedlings in a tower and learn the basics of nutrient water, pH, and light (45 minutes). We wrap up with a tasting of tower-grown greens and herbs, plus tips for growing at home (15 minutes). Every participant takes home a small seedling starter kit. No need to bring anything — just wear comfortable clothes. We provide all materials.
