## Content update
The uploaded content-recommendation document has been applied across the site wherever the content was sufficiently defined.

## Preserved prior decisions where the recommendation document conflicted
1. Public tower lease wording remains **from ¥8,000/month, depending on tower type, location, and setup**. The recommendation document repeatedly proposed ¥10,000/month, but the previously approved public-facing starting price was ¥8,000/month.
2. The Events hub remains streamlined: upcoming events plus past highlights. Registration and payment stay on each event-detail page, and the Events FAQ was not restored because it had previously been removed.
3. The Contact page remains streamlined. It keeps the inquiry form, compact direct-contact information, and Additional Links for summer-camp registration and seedling ordering. It does not restore separate “What are you interested in?” or “Before you contact us” content sections.
4. Offices & Coworking and Residential Buildings remain canonical Green Spaces subpages. Partnerships may cross-link to them but does not duplicate them.
